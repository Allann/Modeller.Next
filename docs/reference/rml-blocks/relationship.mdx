---
title: relationship block
description: Declare a relationship between entities.
---

A `relationship` is valid inside an `entity`. It requires `target` and `cardinality`. Cardinality is `one` or `many`.

```text
relationship Lines
  target "Orderline"
  cardinality many
end
```

Add `optional` when the relationship is not required.

A `relationship` on an entity that declares a `lifecycle` can also add one or more `stage "Name"` clauses, scoping it to specific stages — see [field: Scoping a field to a lifecycle stage](/docs/reference/rml-blocks/field#scoping-a-field-to-a-lifecycle-stage) for the same mechanism and its rules.
