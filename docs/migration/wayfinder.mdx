title: Wayfinder issues
description: The issue contract used to reconstruct each Modeller capability.
---

A Wayfinder issue connects product intent to implementation evidence. It should
be understandable without first learning the shape of the legacy source tree.

## Required sections

1. **[Outcome](/docs/concepts/ubiquitous-language#outcome)** — the user-visible
   or system-visible [capability](/docs/concepts/ubiquitous-language#capability).
2. **Why now** — why this belongs in the next reconstruction slice.
3. **Interface** — everything a caller must know, including invariants and errors.
4. **Acceptance examples** — concrete inputs and observable results.
5. **Legacy evidence** — relevant code, tests, documents, and known
   [behaviour](/docs/concepts/ubiquitous-language#behaviour).
6. **Disposition** — what will be reused, adapted, rewritten, or retired.
7. **Documentation impact** — canonical pages that must change when accepted.

An issue is complete only when the successor behaviour is verified at the new
module seam and its documentation describes the accepted result.
